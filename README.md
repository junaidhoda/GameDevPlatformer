The game concept that we chose is Pathway 1: thrill seeker specifically concept 2

Platformer

Desc: “The player takes on the role of an underwater explorer in search for treasure in this first-person 3D platformer. They have to use platforms and underwater creatures to navigate the depths, avoid the obstacles and hazards, and find all of the hidden treasures. How fast can they complete the levels, and how far can they get? “


Required features:

For each required feature we need to break it down into smaller features like this!

➢ The player can move forward, backward, rotate left/right, strife
left/right, crouch, jump, sprint. Movement suggests water
resistance. No swimming.

Basic movement.

1.1: Being able to dash in the air and on ground for a quick burst of movement.
1.2: the bursts have a stamina bar that come back with time.
1.3: there are items that restore part of your stamina bar instantly. 

➢ At least 2 levels with different challenges for the player. The end of
the second level includes an area where the player’s oxygen tank
breaks and they must use air bubbles to cross.

2.1: There are different hidden objects on each level for the player to find (hidden treasure/shipwreck) etc.
2.2: Could have time-based challenges, awarding different number of stars/medals based on how quickly the player completes the level
2.3: Different difficulties perhaps, which varies the number and health of the enemies


➢ The levels are made up of platforms of different types: solid,
wobbly/slippery, one-time use, jump pads, moving etc.

3.1: adding temporary objects that disappear after they are stood on. platforms.
3.2: For example could use a large shell/old breaking ship that breaks if it is stood on for too long.
3.3: Can use other debris/friendly fish you would find in the sea to act as the platforms


➢ There are also many traps of different types: spiky fish, exploding
fish, sticky trash that adds to the player’s weight or affects vision etc.

4.1: Occasionally we have some places between fights and platforming that would have like arrows that shoot across the screen that the player must avoid.
4.2: We can have also sharks that follow us through certain themes and levels until we finish that level sort of like a time chase thing!
4.3: There could be an old booby trap from a shipwreck which shoots an arrow at the player from a tripwire or something that triggers it (could be a movement-sensitive trap)

➢ Checkpoints. Player position is reset to the start or the last
checkpoint if they fall off platforms or get harmed by traps.

5.1: Checkpoints are placed throughout the levels, allowing players to save their progress when they reach certain areas.
5.2: If the player falls off a platform or gets killed by an enemy or trap, they respawn at the last checkpoint they activated.
5.3: Checkpoints change colour or glow when activated so the player knows their progress has been saved. 
5.4: Players have the option to restart the current level from the beginning if they want to improve their completion time.


➢ Treasure pieces are scattered around the level. The player
progresses to the next level if they collect all, and they win if they
finish the last level. Optional treasure for bonus points appears in
places hard to reach.

6.1: Treasure pieces are scattered throughout levels, with some hidden in harder to reach areas to encourage exploration  
6.2: A counter on the screen shows how many required treasure pieces the player has collected and how many are remaining 
6.3: Collecting all required treasure unlocks the exit to the next level, while completing the final level displays a victory screen. 
6.4: Optional treasure can be collected for bonus points, encouraging players to take more difficult routes though the level.

Bonus point system

➢ There are swimming creatures of different types controlled by AI.
Some help the player move between platforms, or bring them
treasure. Some hurt the player instead and add to the dangers the
players must avoid. Stealth is occasionally required to avoid
detection and dodge the attacks of the larger creatures.

7.1: Friendlier sea creatures, such as turtles/dolphins help the player progress by being able to stand on the shell of the turtle or ride the back of the dolphin
7.2: Sharks would be chasing the player and if they get caught it is instant death
7.3: Other creatures such as pufferfish/eels or other more dangerous sea creatures deal damage to the player

Stealth based interactiosn.

➢ Evolving levels: the game gets harder or easier depending on player
Performance.

8.1: the boss fights become harder in terms of the damage and their time to kill you decreases.
8.2: the platforms become more trapped and have higher variance of these platforms that make navigating your levels much harder.
